SAMPLE LEASE — FICTIONAL, FOR DEMONSTRATION ONLY

Tenant rents Apartment 3B at 100 College Avenue, State College, Pennsylvania, from August 15, 2026 through August 14, 2027. Monthly rent is $1,200, due on the first day of each month. A $75 late fee applies after the third day. The security deposit is $1,200.

Tenant must give 90 days' written notice before the end of the term. If no notice is received, the lease automatically renews for another 12 months at the then-current rate.

Subleasing is prohibited without the landlord's prior written consent. The landlord may withhold consent at its sole discretion.

Tenant is responsible for all repairs, including plumbing, heating, and appliances, regardless of cause. The landlord may enter the unit at any time without notice.

Tenant pays electricity, internet, and a $250 nonrefundable move-in administration fee. The lease does not specify when the security deposit will be returned.
